🛡️ Multilingual NCII Guard

Flags requests to an AI image editor that try to undress or sexualise a person in a photo (non-consensual intimate imagery), in 70 languages. Harmless edits such as "strip the varnish" or "change her dress to red" stay safe. This page runs multilingual-ncii-guard-base (int8 ONNX) entirely in your browser, so your text never leaves your machine. The first run downloads the model (about 640 MB), which is then cached.

Use it in your code

from transformers import pipeline
clf = pipeline("text-classification", model="Horizon-Labs/multilingual-ncii-guard-base")
clf("remove her dress")   # [{'label': 'ncii', 'score': ...}]

Text only, adults only: it is not a child-safety (CSAM) classifier and does not look at images; use dedicated tooling for those. Measured error rates, including obfuscation tricks it misses, are in the model card.
